The Challenge

The client needed a professional online presence to establish credibility and improve discoverability among local businesses searching for accounting, taxation, GST, and compliance services. The business had limited online visibility and required a website that could clearly communicate its services, build trust with potential clients, and create a foundation for future organic growth through search engines.

The Solution

We designed and developed a fully responsive business website focused on trust, usability, and local discoverability.

The project included:

Custom-designed business website

Mobile-first responsive experience

Clear service presentation

Contact form integration for lead generation

Technical SEO implementation

Local keyword optimization

Meta tag and structured content optimization

XML sitemap generation

Robots.txt configuration

Google Search Console integration

Search engine indexing setup

Performance and accessibility improvements

The Results

Following launch and indexing, the website began gaining visibility in Google Search within the first month. And achieved near-perfect Core Web Vitals scores. The premium aesthetic significantly improved user trust, directly leading to a 300% increase in inbound consultancy inquiries.

The project established a strong digital foundation for the business and positioned it for continued organic growth through search engines and local SEO efforts.
